# Sun Street design system

## Direction

The horizon is the interface. The sunrise mark is not a logo pasted onto a page; its line, arc, light and sense of forward movement shape the whole experience.

The site should feel like a precise Hong Kong practice with range across APAC: calm, senior, international and quietly optimistic. It must never read like a generic consultancy template or a dashboard made of cards.

## Brand constants

- Sunrise mark and `SUN STREET` wordmark
- D-DIN for all typography
- Deep navy `#011E41`
- Sun gold `#C9A84C`, with `#E4C86D` reserved for light-on-dark emphasis
- Hong Kong origin, 2016 founding year and APAC operating context
- Three disciplines: Consulting, Trading and Coaching

## Visual language

- Full-bleed compositions, editorial grids and deliberate asymmetry
- Horizon lines, arcs, coordinates and large typography used as structure
- Warm porcelain surfaces instead of sterile white everywhere
- Hairline rules instead of rounded containers and shadowed cards
- One strong visual idea per section
- Photographs only when real, identity-specific imagery exists; never use generic stock

## Typography

- Display: D-DIN Regular at large scale with tight tracking and compact line height
- Body: D-DIN Regular, 16px minimum, 1.5–1.7 line height
- Labels: D-DIN Bold, 11–12px, generous tracking, uppercase
- Italics carry the human or directional emphasis; they should be sparse
- Headings use balanced wrapping and body copy uses pretty wrapping

## Motion

- Motion supports atmosphere or wayfinding: sunrise, horizon draw, content reveal, directional hover
- Interactive changes use interruptible transitions
- Enter motion stays between 500–700ms; hover motion stays between 180–300ms
- Never animate layout properties
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` across smooth scrolling, scroll progress and content animation
- Content must remain understandable and complete with all motion removed

## Accessibility

- Body copy must meet WCAG AA contrast
- Interactive targets are at least 44×44px
- Every route has one `h1`, an active-navigation state and a skip link
- Focus-visible rings use sun gold
- Mobile layouts are composed for touch and narrow scanning, not merely stacked desktop grids

## Anti-patterns

- Three equal cards with borders, radius and shadows
- Low-opacity type used as body copy
- Decorative motion with no hierarchy purpose
- Repeated page templates that make the three disciplines indistinguishable
- Hidden content that only appears after scrolling
- Testimonials in carousels without a narrative reason
